## Microsoft Store
Tags: commercial work, installation. Link: martinagency.com

Working with an amazing team of creatives, designers, animators, producers and technical people, I was a part of launching one of the most advanced stores in the world. ## Birds Installation
Tags: art, installation, personal work, print, processing. Links: patricksimkins.com, vimeo.com/8058125, vimeo.com/9208841

Every month in Richmond, several galleries open up to different local artists exhibits. Patrick Simkins and I collaborated on some print work and an installation involving a digital bird flocking simulation. We added a little interactivity by using a web camera to look for glow sticks that the birds reacted to like food. We also used a mic to measure noise around the installation, and if it got too loud, the birds would get scared and fly away for a moment. You can also see some early test renderings here and here.

## Light of Human Kindness
Tags: art, installation, personal work, processing. Link: thelightofhumankindness.com

The Light of Human Kindness project was a rare instance several artists and technologists were able to collaborate together to make something that not only looks beautiful, but has special meaning. This project started with painting an abandoned building, and filling it with words of kindness that have taken place in peoples lives. After that, a elaborate mural was painted over top, while still showing a lot of the original words. The final layer was to hang 1,000 high-output LED pods across the wall, and they were controlled with 2 experimental light controllers and a single computer. If you go to the site you can upload an act of kindness, and in turn one of the LED pods will light up in real time. To take the idea of kindess even further, we integrated conductive paint spots into the mural so when people join hands and touch more than 1 at a time, the animation of the lights will change. And the more people holding hands, the cooler the animation.

## Lorenz Attractor
Tags: art, personal work, print, processing. Links: Wikipedia (Lorenz attractor), processing.org, source download (lorenz.zip)

I've never been great at freehand drawing, so I quickly fell in love with generating art with code. The Lorenz Attractor was an early chaos algorithm I played with to do some of this code-generated art. I've since created a tool in Processing that allows you to manipulate the variables and general algorithm at runtime and then output a vector PDF that you can bring into Illustrator or PhotoShop. These few prints are the results. Extra: The tool itself is open source, and available for download here.

## Coffitivity OSX App
Tags: obj-c, OSX, personal work. Links: coffitivity.com, Mac App Store (id659901392)

Coffitivity is an online service that provides ambeint noise to mimic that of a coffee shop. It's been shown to increase focus and productivity. But browsers have some limitations when playing audio, so I built a native OSX app for a better experience for Mac users. The app has a simple interface that all takes place in the menu bar. You can have it launch on startup, and even set a preference so that just clicking on the icon will stop and play the audio. It has been featured in several publications, and eventually rose to be #11 overall in free apps.

## English Tower
Tags: aws, commercial work, game, international, jquery, php, social. Link: english-tower.com

English Tower is word-search game designed to make discovering new english words fun. Every time you pass a level, you build another floor of your English Tower. You can see where you rank worldwide, or with just your friends, and you can even challenge them to beat your score on a particular board. And floors aren't added just from passing levels, you can also find hidden floors throughout the game by finding particular words or sequences. At last check, the global leader's tower was over 400 floors high. All the boards are dynamically generated and sorted by difficulty. We also used web APIs to generate dynamic definitions as well as MP3s for pronunciation.

## Color Minutes
Tags: art, jquery, mobile, music, personal work. Links: empressof.me, cargocollective.com/briancrandall

In October 2012, Brooklyn-based musician Empress Of posted 15 one-minute songs on YouTube with 15 flat colors as the only visual accompaniment. ## My Story
Tags: aws, commercial work, flash, international, php, social

How do you inspire parents from all over the world to believe that with english, their child can achieve anything? You show them the future. My Story allows anyone to upload a photo (or use their web cam) to create a realistic movie showing the possibilities of a persons life over the next 20 years. In just under a year, over 2.5 million videos have been created and 7.5 million have been watched. We used Amazon cloud services to help scale the site as we launched in 16 countries world wide.

## Screaming Fast
Tags: commercial work, flash, game, social

BFG, a professional tire manufacturer, asked The Martin Agency to deliver the experience of a drag race without being there, so we brought the most exciting part to the fans, the screaming. The concept was straight forward, but hard to believe. You yell at the computer screen in order to drive your car to the finish line, and the louder you yell, the faster your car goes. You even have to "let off the gas" to change gears.

## EML Labs
Tags: flash, personal work, php

## Life in
Tags: api, commercial work, international, jquery, mobile, node.js, php, social

Education First has over 50 schools around the world each with their own stories to tell, and who else better to tell those stories than the students. Life in was created to help students inspire one another with photos and reviews of places around the city of their school. All content is not only 100% student contributed, but is also self-moderated. As the largest scoped project I've ever worked on, here are a few of the key features: Facebook app, Fully RESTful API, Web and mobile versions of site, Dynamic translation of all content, Organic ranking of Top 100.
