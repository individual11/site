When Neil Armstrong died, the JFK Library wanted to let everyone he inspired take him back to the moon. We built One Small Tweet to collect the memorial tweets as they came in, and every one moved the journey a step closer.

People posted from all over the world, and 2,481 tweets later the rocket made it.
