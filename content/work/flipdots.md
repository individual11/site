I started the Martin Lab, The Martin Agency's emerging-tech unit. One of the first things we found was a leftover prop from a BFGoodrich shoot sitting in our basement: a 50 x 28 flip-dot board. We rebuilt it from the ground up and turned it into a creative tool.

Ruby drives the board over serial. A Node web controller queues up animations. Dots Maker, a desktop app, lets you draw and animate frames with onion skinning and a shape library (space invaders included). There's also multiplayer Pong you play from your phone.
